## Role of this repository

DMP is the decision memory and irreversibility governance layer.

It asks:

```text
What was decided, why, and did later reality make the decision irreversible?
```

DMP preserves consequence-bearing governance memory instead of allowing decisions to disappear into retrospective narrative revision.

## Ecosystem position

```text
PythiaLabs — pre-execution evidence gates for high-risk agentic actions
LTP — path-level trace/replay/admissibility protocol
CML — causal permission and responsibility lineage
DMP — decision memory and irreversibility governance
LRI — living identity and relational invariants
```

## How DMP connects to the other layers

| Layer | Relationship |
|---|---|
| PythiaLabs | DMP can provide escalation signals when a proposed action may become irreversible or governance-relevant. |
| LTP | LTP preserves the path-level trace that led to a decision or action. |
| CML | CML explains causal permission and responsibility lineage for the decision/action. |
| LRI | LRI adds human identity, agency, and revisability boundaries when decisions affect living persons. |

## Reviewer interpretation

DMP should not be read as an ordinary decision log.

An ordinary decision log records what was chosen. DMP preserves the decision context, reversibility assumptions, observed outcomes, supersession, and irreversibility signals.

## Non-claims

DMP does not claim:

- full AI alignment;
- production governance certification;
- regulatory compliance certification;
- replacement of legal, compliance, or human governance teams;
- automatic resolution of irreversible-risk decisions.

The narrower claim is:

```text
DMP is an open-source protocol for preserving consequence-bearing decision memory, reversibility assumptions, and irreversibility signals.
```
