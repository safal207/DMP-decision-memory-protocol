# DMP Non-Claims

Status: reviewer-facing scope boundary.

This document explains what DMP does not claim.

## Purpose

DMP is intentionally narrow.

It contributes one focused primitive:

```text
consequence-bearing decision memory under reversibility and irreversibility risk
```

The project is strongest when its claims remain specific, reproducible, and evidence-backed.

## What DMP claims

DMP claims that important decisions can be represented with durable governance-memory fields such as:

- decision context;
- rationale;
- alternatives considered or rejected;
- reversibility assumptions at decision time;
- observed outcomes;
- irreversibility signals;
- supersession links instead of silent rewriting;
- boundary-aware review signals.

The core question is:

```text
What was decided, why, and did later reality make the decision irreversible?
```

## What DMP does not claim

DMP does not claim:

- full AI alignment;
- complete governance automation;
- certified compliance;
- production governance certification;
- legal admissibility;
- automatic legal, policy, or ethical correctness;
- automatic resolution of irreversible-risk decisions;
- replacement of human governance;
- replacement of legal, compliance, safety, or ethics teams;
- replacement of security review;
- prediction of all future consequences;
- prevention of all bad decisions;
- universal agent safety;
- medical, legal, financial, clinical, or investment advice.

## Not just a decision log, but not a governance oracle

DMP is more than an ordinary decision log because it preserves reversibility assumptions, observed outcomes, supersession, and irreversibility signals.

But DMP is not a governance oracle.

It does not decide what is morally, legally, or strategically correct by itself.

It helps preserve the evidence and structure needed for later review.

## Not a compliance product

DMP is not currently certified for regulatory, legal, or compliance use.

It can support governance review, but it should not be represented as certified compliance infrastructure.

## Not legal admissibility

DMP records may support audit reasoning, but the protocol does not make records legally admissible by default.

Legal admissibility depends on jurisdiction, evidence handling, institutional process, chain of custody, and other factors outside this protocol.

## Not automatic truth discovery

DMP preserves what was recorded as rationale, context, assumption, outcome, or review signal.

It does not automatically prove that the recorded rationale was truthful, complete, or wise.

## Not a substitute for human review

DMP is designed to make human review harder to bypass, rewrite, or forget.

It does not replace human judgment.

In high-impact cases, DMP should make review easier and more accountable, not remove it.

## Benchmark and validation limitation

The current validation surface is deterministic and scaffolded.

It is useful as seed evidence that the schema, examples, and validation scripts behave consistently.

It does not prove broad real-world governance performance.

Reviewer-safe validation statement:

```text
DMP includes deterministic examples, schema validation, tests, and tracked validation results for its current protocol artifact.
```

Do not inflate this into a claim of real-world governance reliability.

## Correct funding framing

The strongest funding framing is:

```text
DMP is an open-source protocol for preserving consequence-bearing decision memory, reversibility assumptions, and irreversibility signals.
```

It should be funded to expand:

- example coverage;
- validation fixtures;
- external review;
- irreversibility taxonomy;
- integration with LTP/CML/PythiaLabs;
- reviewer-facing reproducibility;
- governance-memory benchmarks.

## Bottom line

DMP is not trying to decide everything.

It is trying to make one missing governance layer durable:

```text
decision memory under reversibility and irreversibility risk
```
