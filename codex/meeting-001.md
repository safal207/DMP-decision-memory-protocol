# Codex Session 001

**Date:** 2024-01-01
**Focus:** Establishing DMP as a memory protocol, not a tooling project.

## Notes
- DMP starts with meaning and responsibility.
- The protocol must define what is worth remembering before deciding how to store it.
- Decision records are immutable artifacts; change is captured through superseding records.
- The protocol should remain minimal to avoid conflating decisions with execution.

## Outcomes
- Codex v0.1 drafted.
- Initial decision tiers defined (impact, reversibility, cross-cutting).
- A minimal decision record specification agreed upon.

## Open Questions
- How formal should decision tiers become over time?
- What constitutes acceptable evidence for decision context?
