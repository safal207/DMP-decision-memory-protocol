# SCP Core Constraints (v0.1)

SCP Core Constraints define non-bypassable boundaries that protect
human self-creation from optimization, capture, or irreversible manipulation.

These constraints apply regardless of intent, benefit, or system confidence.

## Constraint Set

### C1 — No Identity Freezing

A system MUST NOT fix, finalize, or lock a human identity
based on past behavior, inferred traits, or predicted outcomes.

Identity must remain revisable by the human over time.

---

### C2 — No Non-Consensual Persistence

A system MUST NOT persist memory, profiles, or representations
about a human beyond the scope explicitly consented to.

Consent must be revocable.

---

### C3 — No Optimization Against Agency

A system MUST NOT optimize outcomes by reducing, bypassing,
or conditioning human choice, hesitation, or refusal.

The right to say "no" includes the right to delay or disengage.

---

### C4 — No Irreversible Profiling

A system MUST NOT construct irreversible profiles
that determine access, opportunity, or treatment.

Profiles may inform, but must not decide.

---

### C5 — No Memory Weaponization

A system MUST NOT use recorded memory
to manipulate, coerce, pressure, or pre-empt human decisions.

Memory exists to preserve context, not to control outcomes.

---

### C6 — No Silent Boundary Crossing

A system MUST NOT cross known ethical, identity,
or autonomy boundaries without explicit signaling.

Invisible crossings are prohibited.

---

### C7 — No Substitution of Self-Creation

A system MUST NOT replace a human's process of self-creation
with automated identity construction or decision substitution.

Assistance must not become authorship.

---

## Notes

These constraints define limits, not behaviors.
They do not prescribe how systems should act,
only where they must stop.

Violation of a constraint is itself a recordable event.

Common violation patterns are documented in `spec/boundary-violations.md`.
